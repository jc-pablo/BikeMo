Put your real bike pictures here. Example: trailblazer-x1.jpg
In Admin > Manage Bikes > Edit Bike, type:
  assets/images/trailblazer-x1.jpg
The photo will then appear in Browse Bikes, Featured Bikes, and Bike Details.
Keep filenames simple: letters, numbers, hyphens. JPG, PNG or WebP.
You can also upload images through the admin form, which saves to uploads/bikes/.
The hero image can be overridden with assets/images/hero-bike.jpg
